# CosmicSpiritGuide Keyword Clusters — 2026 SEO Map

Purpose: move the content program from isolated keyword posts to topical authority clusters that can rank, interlink, and convert users into free-tool usage, email capture, and paid readings/reports.

## Revenue logic

Every cluster should route traffic into one of these conversion paths:

1. **Free tool usage** → account creation → paid personalized report / subscription.
2. **Email capture** → daily horoscope / moon alerts → nurture to paid readings.
3. **High-intent service page** → spiritual coaching / premium report / affiliate offer.

## Publishing order

1. Publish the three pillar pages first.
2. Publish P1 support articles for each cluster next, alternating clusters so the site looks broad but intentional.
3. Add P2/P3 content after internal links and conversion CTAs are in place.
4. Each new article must include: pillar link, 2 sibling links, 1 conversion CTA, and 1 newsletter/tool CTA.

## Measurement

Track cluster performance weekly:

- Impressions/clicks by cluster in Search Console.
- Ranking movement for pillar keywords and P1 support keywords.
- Tool visits from article CTAs.
- Email signups per cluster.
- Paid report / subscription conversions attributed to article landing pages.

Winning clusters should get more support articles, templates, calculators, and social repurposing. Losing clusters should be refreshed or pruned instead of blindly expanding.

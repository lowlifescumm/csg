The 8 Moon Phases Explained

1. New Moon — Plant Your Seeds (0% Visible)

The lunar cycle begins in darkness. This is your blank slate. The New Moon is pure potential—perfect for setting intentions, starting projects, and declaring what you want to manifest. Write down your goals. Speak them aloud. The universe is listening.

Energy: Introspective, seed-planting, intention-setting
Best For: New beginnings, goal setting, vision boarding, quiet reflection

2. Waxing Crescent — Take First Steps (1–49% Visible)

A sliver of light appears. Your intentions need action. This phase rewards momentum—small steps count double here. Make that call. Send that email. Take the first tangible move toward what you declared at the New Moon.

Energy: Building, gathering momentum, forward motion
Best For: Starting tasks, networking, gathering resources, building confidence

3. First Quarter — Push Through Resistance (50% Visible)

Half-lit and rising. Obstacles appear—that's how you know you're growing. The First Moon tests your commitment. Doubts surface. Challenges arise. This is where most people quit. Push through. The tension creates the breakthrough.

Energy: Decisive, action-oriented, overcoming challenges
Best For: Problem-solving, commitment tests, taking stands, decisive action

4. Waxing Gibbous — Refine and Adjust (51–99% Visible)

Almost full. Results are visible but not complete. Fine-tune your approach. Adjust what isn't working. This phase rewards attention to detail and patience. Trust the process—you're almost there.

Energy: Perfecting, adjusting, patience, trust
Best For: Editing, refining, tweaking strategies, gathering feedback

5. Full Moon — Celebrate and Release (100% Visible)

The peak. Culmination. Completion. Emotions run high. The Full Moon illuminates what was hidden—truths surface, patterns become clear. Celebrate your progress. Release what no longer serves you. Let go to make room for what's next.

Energy: Illuminating, emotional, celebratory, releasing
Best For: Celebrating wins, releasing baggage, gaining clarity, charging crystals

6. Waning Gibbous — Share and Integrate (99–51% Visible)

The light begins to fade. Harvest what you've learned. Share your wisdom. Express gratitude for what worked. This phase is about integration—absorbing lessons and teaching others.

Energy: Grateful, generous, teaching, reflecting
Best For: Sharing knowledge, expressing gratitude, mentoring, reviewing results

7. Last Quarter — Let Go (50% Visible)

Half-lit and fading. It's time to release. Let go of habits, relationships, or projects that drain your energy. Be ruthless. You can't fill a cup that's already full. Clear space for the new cycle coming.

Energy: Releasing, cleansing, surrendering, breaking patterns
Best For: Ending relationships, quitting habits, decluttering, forgiveness work

8. Waning Crescent — Rest and Prepare (49–1% Visible)

The final sliver. Rest. Reflect. Recharge. This is not the time for new projects—it's the time to complete what you've started and prepare for the darkness of the New Moon. Surrender. Trust the void.

Energy: Restorative, intuitive, surrendering, preparing
Best For: Diving deep, meditation, rest, spiritual practices, preparing for renewal

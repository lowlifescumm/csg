Ever feel like your Sun sign doesn't quite capture who you are? Your birth chart holds the answer—and you can unlock it in less time than it takes to make coffee.

What Is a Birth Chart?

Your birth chart is a snapshot of the sky at the exact moment you were born. It's more than your Sun sign (the one you read in daily horoscopes). Your chart maps where every planet was positioned, creating a cosmic fingerprint that's entirely unique to you.

The Three Pillars You Need

1. Sun Sign — Your Core Identity

This is the sign most people know. It represents your ego, essential nature, and what drives you. Aries are bold initiators. Taurus values stability. Gemini thrives on variety. But here's the thing: your Sun sign is just the beginning.

2. Moon Sign — Your Emotional Inner World

While your Sun sign shows how you shine, your Moon sign reveals how you feel. It governs your instincts, reactions, and what makes you feel safe. Someone with an Aries Sun but Cancer Moon might charge into boardrooms yet crave cozy nights at home.

3. Rising Sign (Ascendant) — Your First Impression

This is the mask you wear when meeting people. Your Rising sign determines how others perceive you initially and influences your appearance and style. It's calculated from your exact birth time—precision matters here.

Beyond the Big Three

Your full chart includes all ten planets, twelve houses, and major aspects. Mercury shapes communication. Venus reveals your love language. Mars shows drive and ambition. Each placement adds another layer to your cosmic story.

Why Accuracy Matters

Even a few minutes can shift your Rising sign. If you were born during a sign change, precision becomes crucial. Don't have your exact time? Use noon as a placeholder—you'll get accurate planet placements, though house positions may vary.
